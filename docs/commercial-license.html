FAQ

We already pay for the GitHub App (Pro/Business). Do we still need this?+

No. A managed GitHub App subscription includes production use of the hosted service. The commercial license applies only when you deploy and run RevHive yourself (CLI / self-hosted server).

We self-host but only to evaluate. Free?+

Yes — internal non-production evaluation is free under the BSL grant. The moment reviewed code ships to production, buy the Self-Host License.

Students, maintainers of OSS, hobbyists?+

Covered by the free grant. Personal projects, study, teaching and open-source development never need a license.

What do we actually receive?+

A written grant of rights referencing your organization, the license key, activation/deployment notes, and an invoice (CNY or USD). Renewal before expiry keeps updates flowing.

What happens at the Change Date (2030-05-12)?+

The licensed work converts to Apache-2.0 and the license requirement disappears — anyone may use it freely, including in production.

We want to build RevHive into our own product and offer it to our users.+

That's Enterprise territory. Internal production use is covered by the Self-Host License, but distributing RevHive as part of a product you sell requires an OEM grant — talk to us and we'll structure it.

We need a PO / contract / procurement flow.+

That's the Enterprise tier. We'll work through your vendor process and provide the paperwork.
